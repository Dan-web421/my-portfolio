Instructional Design Approach

04

Active learning

Every artifact asks the learner to manipulate a variable — a slider, a cube size, a spectrum point — rather than only read a description.

Multimedia learning

Diagrams, short explanatory text, and interactive controls are placed together so a learner is not forced to hold one in memory while consulting the other.

Visual literacy

Diagrams are built to carry the physical meaning — shrinking size, spreading energy levels — rather than to decorate the page. See the CARP principles.

Formative assessment

Short, low-stakes questions with immediate, explanatory feedback follow each interactive exhibit.

Accessibility

Every interactive element is keyboard-operable and screen-reader friendly; see the dedicated Accessibility page.

UDL principles

Concepts are presented through text, diagram, and interactive manipulation together, offering more than one way to engage with and demonstrate understanding of the same idea.
